# ADR 0005 — Asset sources and no stakes

Original decision: original art/audio, no ripped tracks and no gold/real-money betting or tradeable casino points. Status: Accepted design; the original-art-only source preference is superseded for Warcraft III material by [ADR 0009](0009-warcraft-iii-asset-reuse.md), following the owner's 2026-10-20 direction. The no-stakes rule and exclusion of unrelated commercial racing assets remain accepted and unchanged. Attribution, source/terms records and release review still apply; project source approval is not a claim of third-party copyright permission.
