## Accepted direction and current status

Mirage's closer north-up follow camera fills the canvas using cropped background UVs; it does not require a larger texture or more regions. ## Precomposed track pipeline

1. Keep editable art and PNG masters under `docs/art/concepts/`, outside the installed addon. Retain source layers, attribution and export settings so the map can be revised without repainting runtime files.
2. Compose static terrain, the road, markings, buildings and fixed scenery into `Background.tga`. Use optional `Foreground.tga` only for deliberate occlusion above racers, and `Preview.tga` for track selection. Keep vehicles, interactive props and VFX separate and pooled. Start with one background and an optional foreground, without introducing a tiled-world framework.
3. Author against explicit map bounds and register road edges, walls, gates and spawns in separate Lua geometry. Painted pixels never supply collision or checkpoint decisions; simulation stays deterministic at fixed steps. Export a common coordinate origin, dimensions and transform with the art.
4. A square PNG master at 1024×1024 or 2048×2048 is a candidate authoring format. The current scenery-v1 export is an uncompressed 1024×1024 RGBA32 TGA: its raw pixel payload is 4 MiB per image, excluding headers, mipmaps and runtime overhead. This is not a verified client limit. Two such layers would carry 8 MiB of raw pixels before sprites and previews.
5. Preserve the authored aspect ratio with a uniform world-to-canvas transform. Mirage play uses scale 1.5 and a clamped north-up camera with 60 world units of heading look-ahead; cropped background UVs fill the 800×480 canvas. Positions and dimensions share the transform, with gate/wall regions clipped to the visible rectangle. The whole-map 0.46875 fit remains an authoring overview, not the play camera.
6. Validate texture loading, alpha, dimensions, UV orientation and rotation in the actual installed client before promoting exports. Opaque whole-map loading/orientation is observed in the owner's screenshot; the close camera's static crop and marker alignment/clipping were separately observed on build 70450. Camera motion/edges, transparent sprites and production-facing/rotation remain **NOT RUN**. Keep file paths/casing and an asset manifest explicit; claim performance only from measured results.

## Vehicle sprites

The planned eight archetypes preserve both tank variants: **Siege Tank**, **Steam Tank**, **Goblin Mortar Trike**, **Gyrocopter**, **Gryphon Rider**, **Gnome Drag Car**, **Goblin Drag Car**, and optional **Shredder**. They began as a concept roster, not balance classes. On `claude/track-ui` the Steam Tank, Goblin Mortar Trike, Gyrocopter, Gryphon Rider and Goblin Drag Car, plus a Dwarven Biker, are now selectable cosmetic karts with identical handling (below); the Siege Tank, Gnome Drag Car and Shredder remain planned.

For a single sprite rotated by the UI, use a strict 90-degree overhead orthographic view, front facing north, with neutral steering and a consistent pivot/scale. A Warcraft III-like angled three-quarter view needs directional frames; rotating one angled billboard does not create a valid view from every heading. Test the chosen orientation/UV mapping rather than assuming it matches the current placeholder.

**First runtime sprite (2026-10-20, `claude/track-ui`; retired 2026-10-23):** `Assets/Karts/GoblinDragCar.tga` was an original, procedurally painted 64×128 straight-alpha TGA drawn as a 20×40 world-unit quad. Its visible body stays inside the 17×31 footprint. `Presentation.KART_SPRITES` registers it; UI loads it with a guarded `SetTexture` and keeps the primitive if loading fails. Since 2026-10-21 it is chosen with the Kart button and no longer tinted per phase. Format, reproduction, provenance and the pending client check are in [its asset sheet](art/karts/goblin-drag-car.md). The owner's screenshot shows clean alpha and north facing at spawn; rotation while driving remains **NOT RUN**.

**Goblin Pod Racer (2026-10-23, `claude/podracer-arrows-outline`):** at the owner's request, `Assets/Karts/GoblinPodRacer.tga` replaces the drag car in its slot (id `goblin_drag_car` kept). It is a 1024×1024 sheet of 64 three-quarter headings rendered from World of Warcraft's own goblin rocket car (Blizzard Entertainment), Mirage Raceway's, read from the owner's WoW Forever client by `scripts/wowcasc.py` and `scripts/wowmodel.py`. The drag car's sprite and painter are removed. Every kart sheet also has a half-resolution [outline sheet](art/karts/outlines.md) for the **Outline my kart** setting. See [its asset sheet](art/karts/goblin-pod-racer.md).

**Default kart since 2026-10-20 (`claude/track-ui`):** `Assets/Karts/GoblinMortarTrike.tga` is a 1024×1024 sheet of 64 three-quarter-view headings. It is rendered from the Warcraft III Goblin Mortar Trike model by erwtenpeller and Chriz (Vast Graphics), with no mesh or texture edits, and lit and shadowed like the Mirage scenery. UI shows the nearest heading unrotated, and the Goblin Drag Car stays registered as a single-image kart. [Its asset sheet](art/karts/goblin-mortar-trike.md) records the source, the Read Me terms, the owner's direction, credits and the unresolved redistribution status. The owner [approved it](evidence/2026-10-20-trike-and-shadows-owner-approval.md) as installed on 2026-10-20.

**Kart roster since 2026-10-21 (`claude/track-ui`):** four more 1024×1024 sheets of 64 three-quarter headings, `SteamTank.tga`, `DwarvenBiker.tga`, `GryphonRider.tga` and `Gyrocopter.tga`, are rendered the same way from Warcraft III models of the wc3c ITE project (authors unknown), archived on WC3Vault. The stopped-only Kart button cycles them with the trike and the drag car, and every kart drives the same 17×31 body. The Gryphon Rider and Gyrocopter hover with detached shadows; the Gyrocopter's rotor is an original translucent disc. At the owner's direction, loaded karts are no longer tinted: the drift and boost effects carry the phase. [The roster](art/karts/roster.md) records sources, hashes, stand-ins and the unresolved redistribution status. Client appearance is **NOT RUN**.

Aircraft initially use cosmetic hover and the same ground collision rules as other vehicles. Shadows are separate 2D sprites; no flight physics or altitude-dependent shortcuts are added. Keep drift sparks, exhaust, collisions and celebration VFX in bounded pools, with no per-tick widget creation. The first such pools are the [drift and boost effects](art/effects/drift-boost.md) (2026-10-21, `claude/track-ui`): 38 textures created once, two original atlases, sparks in the meter's tier colours, tyre dust, and exhaust flames and soot while boosting. Client appearance is **NOT RUN**.

## Provenance and sound

Original art and Warcraft III game/community assets are accepted project sources under [ADR 0009](adr/0009-warcraft-iii-asset-reuse.md). Local research, conversion and visual work may proceed without repeating the owner's art-source approval. Prefer referencing suitable textures already present in the installed WoW client rather than bundling copies. Record `asset path | author/source | terms | permission status | date | intended use | modifications/credits` for every imported file, honor community author conditions and retain unresolved rights for release review. Archives, OpenRealm tooling, attribution and another addon's use do not establish a general redistribution grant. Mario Kart/CTR and unrelated commercial racing art/music remain excluded.

Use owned or appropriately licensed audio for engines, pickups, checkpoints, drift charge, impacts and fanfare; honor mute and verify the supported audio API before integration. The source ledger records unresolved candidates without presenting them as rights-cleared assets. Neither the scenery-v1 background nor the kart sprite imports archived models or game textures, and neither establishes production art or milestone acceptance.
