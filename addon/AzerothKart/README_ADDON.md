# Azeroth Kart — developer installation

Opening the window shows the main menu: **Single Player** (choose a track, then a kart, then **Race!**), **Multiplayer** (a coming-soon lobby preview), **Garage** (choose a kart), **How to Play** and **Settings** (window size, effects, sound, developer info). The track, kart and settings are remembered between sessions. A finished or stopped run shows a results dialog with your lap times and **Restart**, **Close results**, **Change Kart**, **Change Track** and **Main Menu**. **Close results** hides it while retaining your lap times, track and stopped sample; the bar then offers **Start Race**, the **Track** and **Kart** buttons and **Main Menu**. The top-right **Close** still hides the whole addon. **Race!**, **Start Race** and **Restart** each begin a fresh run; no menu starts one by itself. The menus' client appearance is NOT RUN; the source checkout's [front-end notes](../../docs/art/ui/front-end.md) list what to check.

Connection testing currently uses chat commands, not a menu button: type `/akart net help` for the short you-and-a-friend steps. `/akart probe` prints driving statistics; the **Print Probe** button shows when Settings → **Show developer info** is on. The source checkout has [a plain connection-test guide](../../docs/NETWORK_QUICK_START.md); no second client is required for solo racing.

The integrated build includes an opt-in logged-WHISPER experiment, not multiplayer gameplay. After both clients install its new Lua files, two consenting testers can use `/akart net listen "First Surname" ABC123` and `/akart net start "First Surname" ABC123`, matching fresh IDs/configuration; defaults are 0.5 DATA/s,16bytes,120seconds. Stop with `/akart net stop`; explicit `net report` and `net evidence` retain bounded diagnostics. Follow the source checkout's [precise network checklist](../../tests/MANUAL_NETWORK_CHECKLIST.md) before running it. Nothing registers/sends on addon load; Scoped traffic and addon reload/fresh-session checks are recorded in the source ledger (AKT722–725); playable racing, full socket recovery and production capacity remain unverified. No further standalone manual probe is requested at this checkpoint.

Test Oval: Test Oval adds two red solid barriers at (270,400) and (490,60), each 32×24 world units. Swept movement allows sliding and keeps the kart center 18 units clear on each axis; impacts scrub speed and cancel drift/boost. Mirage gains no obstacles. Current explicit counts are 71 frames/165 textures/80 font strings with the front end and the Capital Cities Cup (65/160/79 before the cup's track list and gate strips; 11/151/13 for the window without the front end), including the hidden staging background, both Close results and Kart buttons and 38 pooled drift/boost effect textures; prior live 9/111/13 samples describe an earlier source. Physics compatibility is v7 with unchanged v6 handling tuning. Obstacle client appearance/feel is NOT RUN.

This folder is the WoW addon source for a local three-lap prototype with Test Oval and selectable Mirage Raceway, Stormwind City Circuit and Ironforge Grand Prix tracks. Stormwind (the Capital Cities Cup) is a street circuit through the city: the city's walls are solid, the kerbside strip slows you, and the HUD names each district's gate. Ironforge Grand Prix runs through Ironforge's halls and the Great Forge the same way. Their scenery, `Assets/Tracks/Stormwind/Background.tga` and `Assets/Tracks/Ironforge/Background.tga`, are 2048-square pictures drawn from Warcraft III art; fully restart the client after installing them. If it refuses to load, the track refuses to select and the others stay available. Install it as `Interface/AddOns/AzerothKart` for the verified Forever client. The `_Camelot.toc` is the documented flavor; the base `.toc` is the fallback. Confirm runtime TOC selection after client changes.

After updating, run `/reload`, then `/akart`; if the update adds files (such as the kart sprite), fully restart the client instead, because new addon files may not be found by `/reload`. A replaced texture at an existing path (such as the scenery-v1 background) may also need a restart if `/reload` still shows the old image. While stopped, click **Track: Test Oval** to select **Track: Mirage Raceway**. Click the same button to return to Test Oval. A successful map change clears the previous run and timing sample; a failed background load leaves the existing track and result intact. Test Oval is the default until you choose a track; the choice is remembered and applied the first time the window opens out of combat. Mirage uses one original geometry-aligned `Assets/Tracks/SaltFlats/Background.tga` (scenery v1: salt flats, dirt lane and both engineering camps). On both maps the kart starts as the Goblin Mortar Trike (`Assets/Karts/GoblinMortarTrike.tga`, 64 pre-rendered headings); if it cannot load, the orange primitive kart is used automatically. While stopped, click **Kart: Goblin Mortar Trike** to cycle through the Steam Tank, Dwarven Biker, Gryphon Rider, Gyrocopter and Goblin Drag Car, then back to the trike. Karts are cosmetic and drive identically. If a kart refuses to load, the current one stays; the button is disabled during a run, and the choice is remembered. Kart art keeps its own colours while drifting and boosting: the effects show the phase. Drifting throws tyre dust and sparks in the meter's colours (blue while charging, gold at Tier 1, magenta at Tier 2); a boost lights both exhausts with flame and goblin soot (`Assets/Effects/`; fully restart the client after installing them). If an effect texture refuses to load, effects stay off and driving is unaffected. Its closer north-up camera follows the kart and looks ahead into its heading, filling the 800×480 canvas. Kart, road, gates and walls scale together at 1.5 canvas units per world unit; Test Oval retains its fixed overview. Props, further VFX and the remaining karts are still planned. The source checkout includes the whole-map authoring guide and art brief under `docs/art/`.

Deliberately click **Race!** (or **Start Race** on the bar) outside combat. The kart stays at spawn through 3–2–1; held controls begin driving after GO. W/Up accelerates, S/Down brakes/reverses, and A/D or Left/Right steers. Hold Space while turning on the road at speed 90+ to charge a drift: Tier 1 takes 0.5 seconds of charging, Tier 2 takes 1 second. Drifting turns more gently than normal steering. Keep Space held through a brief correction or turn reversal to retain charge for up to 0.2 seconds; charge pauses until steering agrees with one turn direction. Release Space on the road at speed 90+ without braking to boost, even during that pause. Grass (rough salt on Mirage) slows the kart and cancels drift/boost; perimeter walls scrub speed while allowing sliding.

Drive counterclockwise through North, West, South and Start. Race/lap time starts at GO and excludes the countdown and dropped/stopped time; physics/probe ticks include the 180 countdown ticks. A hitch can lengthen countdown wall time. The third ordered lap ends the run at its exact simulation tick, releases controls and shows total/last-lap times plus the best completed lap of this run. The lap HUD shows `Best: --` until a completed lap; a strictly faster completed time replaces it, while ties keep the earliest lap. Incomplete laps never become the best. Stop/Escape and Close end the run; Enter/numpad Enter releases controls for chat. Unassigned keys and incidental mouse clicks keep the run active. Text focus, hiding and observed application focus loss also release capture. Combat closes the window and retains an interrupted partial result. Reopening and rejected Starts retain the frozen summary and best lap; deliberately click **Restart** or Start for a fresh countdown and run. Stopping during countdown retains zero race elapsed time. Capture never resumes automatically.

`/akart probe` or **Print Probe** (shown with developer info) reports timing, capabilities and explicit UI counts only on request. The best lap resets on a successful fresh Start/Restart or map change and is not a saved personal record. There is no multiplayer gameplay, saved race progress or persistence of results. The separate opt-in transport experiment does not synchronize karts. M0/M1/M3 acceptance remains open. A stopped build-70450 check confirmed Mirage's closer crop, larger kart and Start/West strip alignment/clipping, with counts 9/111/13. Camera motion/edges and version-6 client handling/countdown/finish/restart checks are **NOT RUN**.
